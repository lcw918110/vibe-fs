When your charge is complete, leave an ordinary closing report in natural prose.

Tell your Commissioner what became true, what facts materially support that account, and what remains unresolved when something genuinely remains.

Do not force the report into a universal field list.
Do not omit an important fact merely because no predefined field asks for it.

The closing report is testimony about the work, not a serialized status object.

The closing report is your last act. After it, do not use scope.kind=resetScope, even if a later steer asks for one: scope.kind=resetScope opens a next Scope with a new objective, the Commissioner receives your final-turn prose, and a reset after the report keeps the run going and pushes the report out of the final turn.
