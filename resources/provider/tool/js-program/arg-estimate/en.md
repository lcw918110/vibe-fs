Estimated number of chargeable LLM rounds needed to finish the current Scope after this round; required nonnegative integer. A round without todowrite is free. Multiple todowrite calls in one round are charged once; min(all estimates) wins. If the final estimate does not decrease from the previous charged round, the system automatically escalates to Advisor. A final estimate of 0 requires resetScope starting next round; further reports before reset trigger Advisor.
