Entrust an independent road to a Manager within this campaign.

Commission a separate road when the work can proceed independently and has a
coherent destination of its own.
The test is independence of purpose, not size of labor and not position in a
lifecycle.

Do not create another road merely because work is large or has entered a next
stage.
A later stage of the same destination is still the same road.

This is not fork: it does not open another office inside a mission.
This is not a technical phase and not Host admission machinery.

A successful return means the named road has taken the charge.
It does not mean the destination has been reached.

For a new road, pass name + charge; calling is optional and derives to lead.
To continue a known road, omit calling and use the same name.

calling accepts lead: a Manager who directs the road toward its destination,
coordinates its work, and judges completion. This persona does not change Office permissions.
name is the byname of the road.
charge is the destination and constraints, not a prescription of hidden tools.
