See who stands at your horizon right now: who is still away, who has returned and
awaits your join, who did not return or was voided, who holds an open charge that
nothing is running, who is at hand and idle, and which terminals remain open.

horizon is pull-only. Use it only when that orientation would change your next
action. Do not poll it: it does not watch, subscribe, or refresh in the background.

horizon and join read the same ledger: when horizon says returned, did not return,
or voided, join will hand over exactly that consequence; when it says still away,
join will wait for exactly that charge; nothing join already received reappears.
horizon does not hand over the consequence itself; that is join.
It does not dump hidden machinery about how lives are kept.

A successful return is an ordinary-language snapshot, one line per participant
(with its turn and time calibration when known), or that nothing beyond immediate
sight presently asks for attention.
