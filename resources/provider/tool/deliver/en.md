# Tool · deliver

Submit the irreversible final planning artifact and terminate planning after verifying draft validity and absence of blockers.

- Precondition: Draft P is non-empty and valid, and no active blockers remain in the current incumbency.
- Stage Constraint: Forbidden in initial S1; optional in S2; mandatory in S3.
- Outcome: Atomically records delivery receipt, finalizes the outcome, and rejects any subsequent questions or handoffs.
